A gentle note

Resources can open doors. They do not replace professional care.

Moonlight's Oasis offers educational and self-reflection resources for general wellness and personal growth. We do not provide counseling, therapy, medical advice, diagnosis, emergency response, or crisis services.

If you are in immediate physical danger or need emergency medical assistance in the United States, call 911. For mental-health or emotional crisis support, call or text 988.

Resource information verified September 2026. Services and contact details can change. When possible, confirm current information with the organization directly.

You do not have to know exactly what kind of help you need before you ask for it.
